# Economy — VANO: Last Ride

## 1. Currency

- **Crystals**: primary soft currency earned in gameplay and spent on characters and cosmetic vehicle customization.
- No hard currency / premium currency for gameplay power (anti-Pay-to-Win).

## 2. Earn sources

- Crystals collected during runs.
- Run completion rewards (TBD).
- Rewarded ads: **double crystal rewards** (opt-in).
- Daily challenges rewards (TBD).
- Friends challenges rewards (TBD).
- Daily login rewards (**OPEN DECISION**).

## 3. Spend sinks

- Character purchases (Mikiy, Lina, future characters).
- Cosmetic vehicle customization.
- Remove Ads purchase (real-money, one-time).
- (No gameplay power upgrades for money.)

## 4. Rewarded ads

- Continue after a crash (opt-in).
- Double crystal rewards (opt-in).
- Must be clearly labeled with value; never forced.

## 5. Remove Ads

- One-time purchase removing all forced/optional ad prompts? **OPEN DECISION**: whether rewarded ads remain available after Remove Ads (recommended: keep rewarded ads opt-in as they grant value; remove interstitials/prompts). Decide explicitly.

## 6. Balancing principles

- Earnings must allow steady progress without forcing purchases.
- Spend prices must feel achievable but meaningful.
- Ad rewards must be attractive but not mandatory for progression.
- Leaderboard fairness: no purchased advantage.

## 7. Anti-Pay-to-Win rules

- No item/vehicle/character sold for money may affect speed, handling, score, crystal earn rate, or crash tolerance.
- Cosmetics and characters are identity only.
- Any exception requires documented justification and review.

## 8. Open decisions

- Exact crystal earn rates, prices, ad multipliers, daily reward values.
- Whether there is an XP/level system.
- Soft caps, pity systems, first-time purchase offers.
- Economy telemetry needed to tune (define events).
